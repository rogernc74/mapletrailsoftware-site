Private handoff planning

01 · Organize

Organize the essentials.

Keep bills, insurance, household information, digital-life details, wishes, and trusted contacts in clearly labelled categories. Start small and build your plan over time.

02 · Add context

Make the details useful.

Add helpful notes and access information, then review what needs attention. Keep the practical context close to the item it belongs to.

03 · Prepare

Plan for a helping hand.

Create readable handoff PDFs and trusted-person packets when you decide you are ready. You choose what to include and when to share.
