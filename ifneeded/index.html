Why it exists

A calm place for information that is usually scattered.

If Needed is built for practical readiness, not paperwork overload. Start with a simple plan, fill in what matters, and keep the important details findable.

Collect the essentials

Bills, money, insurance, digital life, household details, wishes, and trusted contacts.

Review what needs attention

See missing locations, access notes, stale bills, and items that need a quick check.

Share when ready

Create readable handoff PDFs and trusted-person packets when you decide it is time.

Privacy

Designed around local-first planning.

If Needed can work without a MapleTrail account. Private iCloud sync is optional and uses your own Apple iCloud account when you turn it on.

No ads

No third-party advertising tracking.

Device authentication

Use Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode.

Private export

You choose when to create or share a handoff PDF.

Free to start. Unlock once when your plan grows.
