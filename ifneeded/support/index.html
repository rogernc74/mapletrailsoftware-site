Help for If Needed

If Needed is a private handoff planning app for organizing practical details someone trusted may need if they ever have to step in.

Last updated: April 30, 2026
Support email support@mapletrailsoftware.com

What to include

For faster help, include that you are using If Needed, your device model, iOS or macOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Common support topics

  • Restoring the If Needed Lifetime Unlock.
  • Using private iCloud sync across your own Apple devices.
  • Turning app lock, Face ID, or Touch ID on or off.
  • Creating or sharing a handoff PDF.
  • Understanding the free item limit and one-time unlock.
  • Managing or deleting app data from your device or iCloud settings.

Privacy

If Needed is designed to keep your plan private. Read the app-specific privacy policy at mapletrailsoftware.com/ifneeded/privacy/.

Important note

If Needed helps organize practical information. It is not legal, financial, medical, insurance, tax, or estate-planning advice.

Response time

MapleTrail Software is an independent studio. Support requests are reviewed as quickly as possible.
