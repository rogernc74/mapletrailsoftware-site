Acadie Account

Delete your account and cloud data

You can permanently delete an Acadie Account either inside the app or by sending a request from this page. You do not need continued access to the app to use the request option.

Delete inside Acadie

  1. Open More, then Acadie Account.
  2. Open Data & privacy.
  3. Choose Delete cloud account.
  4. Confirm your identity again with Apple, Google, or your email password.
  5. Confirm permanent deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@mapletrailsoftware.com with the subject Acadie Account Deletion Request. Include:

  • The email address associated with the account, including an Apple private-relay address if that is what Acadie displays.
  • Whether you used Apple, Google, or Email to sign in.

Do not send a password, Apple private key, access token, or verification code. MapleTrail Software will reply with an ownership-verification step. After verification, the request will be completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • The Firebase Authentication account used by Acadie.
  • Cloud-backed roots, notes, settings, progress, deletion markers, and synced-device records under that account.
  • Cloud recovery snapshots associated with the account.

What is not deleted automatically

Local information and downloaded videos remain on each device until removed there or the app is deleted. Deleting an Acadie Account does not cancel or change an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription because storefront purchases are separate from the Acadie Account.
