Acadie Privacy Policy

Your account is optional. Your personal trail remains yours.

This policy explains how Acadie handles information in the app and in the optional Acadie Account.

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Using Acadie without an account

Acadie remains fully usable without signing in. Saved roots, notes, settings, story progress, and downloaded video files stay on your device unless you choose to enable account backup. Video files are not uploaded through Acadie Account backup.

Information used for an Acadie Account

If you create or use an account, Firebase Authentication assigns an account identifier. Depending on the sign-in method you choose, Firebase and Acadie may receive your email address, display name, and an Apple, Google, or email-provider identifier. Apple may provide a private relay email address. Acadie does not receive or store your Apple or Google password, and passwords entered for email authentication are handled by Firebase Authentication.

Information you choose to back up

After you review and confirm the backup preview, Acadie may store your saved roots, notes, settings, app-navigation choices, story progress, deletion records needed for safe synchronization, and basic synced-device records in Cloud Firestore under your Firebase account identifier. A synced-device record contains an app-generated device identifier, platform, and last backup time; it is not a list of all open authentication sessions.

Recovery snapshots may be retained for up to 30 days to protect against an accidental replacement or merge. The app shows a preview before the first merge and does not replace local data with an empty cloud account automatically.

Location, media, and permissions

If you grant location access, your current location is used on the device to help filter nearby discoveries. Acadie does not continuously track or store precise location in your account backup. Downloaded story videos remain local to the device.

Analytics and technical information

Acadie uses Firebase Analytics to understand product interactions such as app launches, opened features, language mode, and support actions. Analytics events are not intended to contain your roots, note text, search text, precise location, account identifier, email address, or downloaded media.

Service providers

Google Firebase provides authentication, account backup, recovery snapshots, and analytics. Apple and Google provide their respective sign-in services when you choose them. Their handling of information is also governed by their own privacy terms.

Sharing and sale

MapleTrail Software does not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the selected features, when required by law, or to protect users and the service.

Retention and deletion

Account data remains until you delete the account or request deletion, subject to limited retention required for security, legal obligations, or resolving a request. In-app account deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account and Acadie cloud records, including recovery snapshots. Local data remains on your device unless you remove it separately.

See Delete an Acadie Account for both the in-app steps and a request method that does not require access to the app.

Your choices

  • Use the full app without an account.
  • Choose Apple, Google, or email authentication.
  • Review the local/cloud preview before enabling synchronization.
  • Export your local personal data from Acadie Account.
  • Sign out without deleting local data or changing a store subscription.
  • Delete your account and cloud data in the app or request deletion online.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@mapletrailsoftware.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as Acadie changes. The revised effective date will appear on this page.
