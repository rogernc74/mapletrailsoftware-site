Acadie · Release notes

See what’s new in Acadie on iPhone, iPad and Android. Release dates can vary by store.

Version 2.0.5

iOS and Android · November 26, 2026

  • Set your email preferences in Acadie and choose which updates you’d like to receive.
  • Give a year of Acadie+ or redeem a gift on the Acadie website. Sign in to your Acadie account to use your gift in the app.
  • Contact Acadie Support directly from the app’s settings.

Version 2.0.4

November 2026

  • Purchase restoration and Acadie+ access are more reliable across iPhone, iPad and Android when you use the same Acadie account.
  • Automatic backups run outside of games to avoid interrupting play.
  • Colouring does a better job of saving your progress when you leave and reopen a picture.

Version 2.0.3

November 2026

  • Comics in New Stories have their own covers. This update also improves performance.

Version 2.0.2

November 2026

  • A side-by-side comparison shows which features are free and which come with Acadie+.
  • Cinematic episode lists make more room for titles and show clearer labels for free and Acadie+ episodes.

Version 2.0.1

November 2026

  • Find other members in Community and visit their homes when they’re open to visitors.
  • Shared links to stories, places and surnames now open directly in Acadie.
  • Games get improvements, and room editing in My Acadie works more reliably.

Version 2.0.0

November 17, 2026

  • Create an avatar, decorate a home and collect items in My Acadie.
  • Play Wheel of Acadie, try the quicker 6×6 daily Sudoku or unwind with Colouring. Sudoku and Wheel of Acadie have daily and weekly leaderboards.
  • Game controls are easier to use, and crashes during account recovery and Community checks have been fixed.

Version 1.6.9

November 12, 2026

  • Acadie+ members can play unranked quizzes from earlier days and resume ones they started. Restoring Acadie+ is also more reliable.

Version 1.6.8

November 11, 2026

  • Memory Match joins the redesigned Games screen with a free daily six-pair puzzle and timed leaderboards. Acadie+ includes extra rounds and past puzzles.

Version 1.6.7

November 10, 2026

  • Games load faster, Trivia, Acadie Code and Word Search have weekly leaderboards, and new genealogy resources help you research your family roots.

Version 1.6.6

November 4, 2026

  • Podcasts keep playing when you lock your screen or leave the podcast page. This update also includes bug and security fixes.

Version 1.6.5

November 3, 2026

  • Acadian comics arrive in Stories, with original French and Chiac titles, clearer previews and improved reading layouts. Names adds surname wallpaper previews.

Version 1.6.1

October 22, 2026

  • Daily Word Search keeps the same official puzzle when you return, and Word Search and Trivia progress stay with the correct account when you switch accounts.

Version 1.6

October 21, 2026

  • Trivia, Acadie Code and Word Search are available from the refreshed Home screen alongside Web Radio. Play to earn points and coins and move up levels.
  • Acadie+ members can prepare extra games for offline play and save their results when they reconnect.

Version 1.5.4

September 29, 2026

  • Acadie Web Radio plays more reliably in CarPlay and Android Auto.

Version 1.5.3

September 28, 2026

  • Listen to Acadie Web Radio in CarPlay and see the current song. This release also improves song titles and artwork and refines the Acadian Creators controls.

Version 1.5.2

September 25, 2026

  • Community names are easier to read. Tap a member’s name or photo to open their profile. Acadian Creators loads more reliably after updates.

Version 1.5.1

September 24, 2026

  • Community comments and search fields are easier to read, a crash after posting a first comment is fixed, and surname search finds the matching family more accurately.

Version 1.5.0

September 24, 2026

  • Listen to Acadie Web Radio with the mini-player, discover Acadian creators and join Community boards about history, families, places, language and traditions.

Version 1.2

September 5, 2026

  • An optional Acadie account keeps roots, notes, preferences and story progress backed up and synchronized. This update also adds data export and controls for synced devices and account deletion.

Version 1.1

August 22, 2026

  • Explore more surnames, family connections, cultural places and stories. This release also improves Canadian French text, captions and content loading.

Version 1.0

August 13, 2026

  • The first App Store release of Acadie introduces a bilingual guide to Acadian stories, places, family names and culture.
