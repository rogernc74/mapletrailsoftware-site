What you can explore

Stories, communities, food traditions, voices, and gatherings.

Acadie is for people exploring heritage, travel, family roots, and cultural context without reducing the experience to a directory.

  • Cinematic stories, narration, subtitles, and reading paths.
  • Communities, events, heritage trails, regions, and cultural places.
  • Acadian surnames, connected history, and personal roots.
  • Food traditions, voices, gatherings, and cultural context.
  • Saved names and places, trail progress, and the Acadian Passport.
