Habit Wallet Privacy Policy

Your goals stay private.

Habit Wallet is a personal habit and reward tracker from MapleTrail Software Inc. It has no public profiles, social feed, or custom account system.

Effective date: July 26, 2026

Information stored by Habit Wallet

Habit Wallet stores the habits, rewards, completion records, redemptions, water logs, settings, and wallet activity you create. This information is used to provide the app's habit, wallet, reward, progress, and history features.

iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, sync-safe wallet records may sync through your personal private iCloud database so they are available on your Apple devices. MapleTrail Software does not operate a separate account or public database for this content.

Apple Health

Habit Wallet requests Health permission only when you choose to connect Apple Health. The app can read supported activity summaries and metrics including steps, active energy, exercise minutes, stand hours, workouts, dietary water, and Activity ring progress to show habit progress and determine completion.

Habit Wallet may write water that you log in the app to Apple Health after you grant write permission. Raw Health samples and exact Health progress are used on your device. They are not sent to Habit Wallet servers, stored in private iCloud records, or included in analytics.

Analytics

Coarse product-usage analytics are enabled by default and can be disabled in Settings. Analytics may describe general feature interactions, but never include habit names, reward names, notes, free-form text, exact Health values, exact water values, or wallet content.

Notifications and device permissions

Habit Wallet requests notification permission only for reminders you choose to use. Health authorization state, notification authorization state, analytics consent, and raw Health data stay on your device.

Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. MapleTrail Software Inc. does not receive your full payment-card details.

Website data

The MapleTrail Software Inc. website does not use a public account system. Basic technical information may be collected by hosting providers or standard server logs to operate and secure the site.

Sharing and tracking

MapleTrail Software Inc. does not sell your personal information. Habit Wallet does not use the advertising identifier, third-party advertising, or cross-app tracking.

Your choices

You can change Health and notification permissions in device settings, disable analytics in Habit Wallet Settings, manage iCloud through your Apple Account settings, and delete app content from within Habit Wallet.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@mapletrailsoftware.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Habit Wallet changes. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.
