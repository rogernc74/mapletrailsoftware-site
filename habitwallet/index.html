Private by design

Your goals are yours.

Habit Wallet has no public profile, social feed, or custom account. Your content stays private, and you stay in control of analytics and permissions.

Private iCloud sync Your wallet data syncs through your personal private iCloud database.

On-device Health progress Raw Health data is not stored in iCloud or sent to analytics.

No personal habit analytics Habit names, reward names, notes, and exact Health values are never included.
