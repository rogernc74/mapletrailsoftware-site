App features

Plan the next place. Keep the whole story.

Moments Away organizes your travel record around places. Save countries, cities, and landmarks; log visits; add notes, people, photos, and videos; then see the bigger picture.

1

Map and remember

Save places from the map, record return visits, and add the notes, people, photos, and videos that give them meaning.

2

Plan what is next

Use the Travel Checklist to keep track of countries and territories you have visited—and the ones you want to visit.

3

See the journey take shape

Review your collection with Year in Travel, Trip Stories, stats, and Home Screen widgets built from your own record.

Privacy and data

Your places and notes are your travel record.

Keep your saved places, visits, notes, and media in a record you control. Use optional iCloud sync across your Apple devices, plus backups and exports for your own copies.

Free and Premium

Your travel record stays free. Premium makes more of it.

The core record—unlimited saved places and moments, visits, notes, photos, videos, basic map and stats, optional sync, backups, and exports—remains free. Premium adds polished ways to review, share, and personalize the travel record you already own.

Premium extras
  • Full Year in Travel reports and share images.
  • Route-based, shareable Trip Stories.
  • The complete customizable Widget Studio.
  • Premium map editions, collector insights, and milestones.
What you can save

Place records with notes, media, and visits.

Use the app for favourite places, quick stops, return visits, road trips, parks, beaches, restaurants, lookouts, and any location you want in your personal travel history.

Included details
  • Place names and map locations.
  • Visit dates and return visits.
  • Notes, people, photos, and videos.
  • Favourites and country grouping.
  • Collection stats, backups, and exports.
