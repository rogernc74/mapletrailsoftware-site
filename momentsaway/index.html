App features

Keep track of places, visits, media, and stats.

Moments Away organizes travel memories around places. Each saved place can include visits, notes, people, photos, videos, favourites, and location details.

Free and Premium

Save your places first. Upgrade for collection extras.

Moments Away includes the core travel record: places, visits, notes, photos, videos, favourites, stats, backups, and exports. Premium adds optional extras for people who want more ways to customize and review their collection.

Premium extras
